## Level 0
### Example 1
**Prompt:** The alphabet is {'a', 'b'}. How many distinct strings of exactly 8 characters match the regular expression [ab]a*(b)*? The answer is a non-negative integer.
**Answer:** 16

### Example 2
**Prompt:** The alphabet is {'a', 'b'}. How many distinct strings of exactly 5 characters match the regular expression a*b*? The answer is a non-negative integer.
**Answer:** 6

## Level 2
### Example 1
**Prompt:** The alphabet is {'a', 'b'}. How many distinct strings of exactly 9 characters match the regular expression a+[b]+b*? The answer is a non-negative integer.
**Answer:** 8

### Example 2
**Prompt:** The alphabet is {'a', 'b'}. How many distinct strings of exactly 7 characters match the regular expression a+[b]+? The answer is a non-negative integer.
**Answer:** 6

## Level 5
### Example 1
**Prompt:** The alphabet is {'a', 'b', 'c'}. How many distinct strings of exactly 12 characters match the regular expression (b)+[bc]+? The answer is a non-negative integer.
**Answer:** 2048

### Example 2
**Prompt:** The alphabet is {'a', 'b', 'c'}. How many distinct strings of exactly 10 characters match the regular expression (b)+c*a*? The answer is a non-negative integer.
**Answer:** 55
