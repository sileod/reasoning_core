## Level 0

Terms are built from variables y, z, constants a, b, c, and functions f, g, h.
Determine whether the two terms below unify. If they do, give the most general unifier's binding for the variable y, written in the same syntax (e.g. f(x,b)).

Term 1: h(h(y),g(z))
Term 2: h(h(y),y)

If they do not unify, answer with exactly "none". Otherwise answer with the term that y is bound to.

Answer: g(z)
