# Deadline scheduling samples

Level 0

### Example 1

On a single machine with no preemption, jobs are scheduled one at a time; a job finishes on time if it completes no later than its deadline. Jobs:
  Job 1: processing time 4, deadline 4
  Job 2: processing time 3, deadline 4
  Job 3: processing time 4, deadline 4
  Job 4: processing time 3, deadline 3
  Job 5: processing time 1, deadline 1
  Job 6: processing time 4, deadline 4
  Job 7: processing time 1, deadline 1
  Job 8: processing time 3, deadline 4
  Job 9: processing time 1, deadline 4
  Job 10: processing time 3, deadline 3
  Job 11: processing time 3, deadline 4
The answer is the largest number of jobs that can all finish on time.

**Answer:** 2
