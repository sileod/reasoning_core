## Level 0

### Example 1

**Prompt:**

An experiment runs in 3 steps. At step 1 a branch is drawn with these probabilities: A (probability 7/22); B (probability 4/11); C (probability 7/22). At step 2 a branch is drawn with these probabilities: A (probability 3/7); B (probability 8/21); C (probability 4/21). At step 3 a branch is drawn with these probabilities: A (probability 1/16); B (probability 1/2); C (probability 7/16). Given that the branch at step 3 is B, what is the conditional probability that the branch at every step is B? Give your answer as a fraction in lowest terms.

**Answer:**

32/231
