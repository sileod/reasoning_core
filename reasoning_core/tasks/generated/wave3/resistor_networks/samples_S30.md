## Level 0

### Example 1

A circuit contains a 2 ohm resistor connected in series with a 12 ohm resistor connected in parallel with a 5 ohm resistor.

What is the equivalent resistance of the whole circuit? Give the answer as an exact fraction in lowest terms, e.g. 7/3.

**Answer:** 94/17

### Example 2

A circuit contains a 4 ohm resistor connected in parallel with a 7 ohm resistor connected in series with a 2 ohm resistor connected in parallel with a 3 ohm resistor.

What is the equivalent resistance of the whole circuit? Give the answer as an exact fraction in lowest terms, e.g. 7/3.

**Answer:** 206/55
