# Samples for distinguishing_witness (P006v1)

## Level 0

### Example 1 (level 0)

A truck has a weight capacity of 28 kg. It carries a group of items, and the group's total weight is the sum of its items' weights.
The available items are: item 0 weighs 34 kg, item 1 weighs 31 kg, item 2 weighs 35 kg, item 3 weighs 37 kg, item 4 weighs 33 kg.

Consider two statements about a group of items:
- Statement F: "The group fits in the truck."
  (true exactly when the group's total weight is at most 28 kg)
- Statement A: "The group weighs at least 109 kg."
  (true exactly when the group's total weight is at least 109 kg)

Because 28 < 109, the two statements can never both be true at once.

Find the smallest nonempty group of items such that the two statements have DIFFERENT truth values (exactly one of them holds). Here 'smallest' means fewest items, and if two groups have the same number of items, take the one whose sorted item indices are lexicographically smallest.
Give the answer as the item indices of that group, sorted ascending, e.g. "item 2, item 3, item 7".

Answer:

item 0, item 1, item 2, item 3
