# Conditional expectation samples (P005v1)

## Level 0

### Prompt

Below is a finite joint distribution over 5 equally numbered outcomes (the probability of each row is its weight divided by 21).
outcome | V1 | V2 | weight
--------------------------
1 | -9 | 0 | 4
2 | -9 | -6 | 6
3 | 4 | 8 | 3
4 | -4 | -3 | 6
5 | 9 | 6 | 2

Restrict to the rows where V1 ≤ 8, renormalize those weights, and compute the conditional expectation of V1 over that restricted distribution.
The answer is the exact reduced rational value a/b, or a single integer if it is whole.
