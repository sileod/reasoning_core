## Level 0

Prompt:
```
A friend texts you for help and asks only for "the sum of 8 and 6." On reflection you realize the request is ambiguous: it could equally mean "the absolute difference of 8 and 6," and the two readings give different numbers.

Ask the single question that resolves which reading is meant, then give the number that follows from each reading. Write the answer as the two integer results, the smaller first, separated by a space (for example: 5 7).
```

Answer:
```
2 14
```
