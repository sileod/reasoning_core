## Level 0
### Example
**Prompt:**

In an invented language the following new words are defined, in the order they will be combined:
  dadi = a metal hook
  gapa = a round stone

To make a compound for something in this language, every modifier is written before the head noun; the parts are joined directly with no linking vowel. The parts are combined in the order listed above.

Write, in this invented language, the form that expresses:
  a round stone that is a metal hook

The answer is that single novel word, in lowercase, with no spaces.

**Answer:**

dadigapa

  nipo = a gravel path

To make a compound for something in this language, every modifier is written after the head noun; the vowel 'o' is inserted between the parts whenever the part that follows starts with a consonant. The parts are combined in the order listed above.
