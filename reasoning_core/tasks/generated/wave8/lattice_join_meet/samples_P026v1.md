## Level 0

### Example 1

**Prompt:**

Given:
a divisibility lattice on the divisors of 16: the elements are [1, 2, 4, 8, 16], ordered by divisibility (x <= y when x divides y), so the join of x and y is their least common multiple and the meet is their greatest common divisor.

Prompt:
Consider the two elements a = 1 and b = 8 of this lattice. What are their join and meet?

The answer is the two integers separated by a single space: the join first, then the meet.

**Answer:**

8 1
