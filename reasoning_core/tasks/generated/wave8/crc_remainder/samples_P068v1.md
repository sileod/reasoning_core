## Level 0
### Example
**Prompt:**
A CRC (cyclic redundancy check) remainder is computed over GF(2) by appending zeros and dividing the message polynomial by the generator polynomial, keeping the remainder. Given the message bitstring 011000110110 and the generator polynomial x^5 + x^4 + 1 (listed in order of descending degree, from x^k down to x^0), compute the CRC remainder bitstring. The answer is a bitstring of length equal to the polynomial degree, most-significant bit first.
**Answer:**
01011
