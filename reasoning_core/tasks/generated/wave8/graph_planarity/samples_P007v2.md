# Level 0

## Example 1

### Prompt

Graph:
Consider the complete bipartite graph K_{6,8}, whose vertices split into two groups of sizes 6 and 8 and every vertex of one group is adjacent to every vertex of the other (no edges within a group).

Genus Note:
The genus of a graph is the minimum number of holes of a surface on which it can be drawn with no edge crossings; a graph is planar iff its genus is 0.

Report the genus of complete bipartite graph.

The answer is a single nonnegative integer (0 means the graph is planar).

### Answer

6
