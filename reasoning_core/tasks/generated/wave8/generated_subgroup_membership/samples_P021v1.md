## Level 0

Prompt:

Work in the additive cyclic group Z_25 (elements 0..24, sum modulo 25). Consider the subgroup generated by a = 20, that is all multiples of 20 modulo 25. The integer g = 5 lies in this generated subgroup. The answer is the smallest positive integer e such that e*a ≡ g (mod 25), written as a plain decimal integer.

Answer:

4
