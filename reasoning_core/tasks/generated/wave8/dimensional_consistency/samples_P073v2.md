## Level 0

### Example 1

**Prompt:**

A has dimension L^-2*T^-2; B has dimension M^3*L^-3*T^3; C has dimension M^-3*L*T^-5; D has dimension L^-2*T^-2; E has dimension M*L*T^3.

In the equation

    4 A + 5 B*C + 2 D = 5 E*X

all terms must have equal dimension for the equation to be dimensionally valid. X appears with power 1 and its dimension is the one missing piece. Express the dimension of X as a vector of exponents (m, l, t) where dim(X) = M^m L^l T^t. The answer is that triple of integers, e.g. (0,1,-1).

**Answer:**

`(-1,-3,-5)`
