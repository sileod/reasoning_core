## Level 0

Balance the following chemical reaction by finding the smallest positive whole-number coefficients for every species. The species appear in this order: C2, Mg2Fe4, Be3Ca2, Be2, C2Mg3Ca2, Fe3.
C2 + Mg2Fe4 + Be3Ca2 -> Be2 + C2Mg3Ca2 + Fe3
Give the coefficients as a comma-separated list of integers, in the order the species are written, with no spaces. Example format: 2,1,3,1

Answer: 2, 3, 2, 3, 2, 4
