### Example 1

**Prompt:**

It starts as EUR 19/50. For each stage in order, apply:
  1. import tariff: any part of the value above EUR 0 and up to EUR 1/5 is charged at 3/5 of that part.
  2. excise duty: any part of the value above EUR 1/5 and up to EUR 29/100 is charged at 3/10 of that part.
  3. sales tax: any part of the value above EUR 29/100 and up to EUR 49/100 is charged at 1/5 of that part.
After the stages, a discount of 3/5 applies, but only if the resulting value is at least EUR 7/25.
Then subtract a fixed rebate of EUR 13/100.
Finally convert to dollars at a rate of 1 EUR = 3/2 USD.

The answer is the final amount in USD, as an exact reduced fraction in the form `n/d`, where n is the integer numerator and d is the integer denominator.

**Answer:**

363/25
