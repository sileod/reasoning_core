## Level 0

### Example 1

**Prompt:**

```
An order book for one good is as follows:
Buyers:
buyer 1 will buy up to 4 units at a price of at most 54 (per unit); buyer 2 will buy up to 5 units at a price of at most 2 (per unit); buyer 3 will buy up to 3 units at a price of at most 34 (per unit); buyer 4 will buy up to 2 units at a price of at most 42 (per unit)

Sellers:
seller 1 will sell up to 1 units at a price of at least 36 (per unit); seller 2 will sell up to 5 units at a price of at least 65 (per unit); seller 3 will sell up to 4 units at a price of at least 96 (per unit); seller 4 will sell up to 2 units at a price of at least 36 (per unit)
Tie-breaking rule: If more than one price clears the same maximum quantity, take the LOWEST such price.
What is the total consumer surplus (sum over buyers of (bid price minus clearing price) times units each actually buys) at the clearing price?
The answer is a single integer.
```

**Answer:** 54
