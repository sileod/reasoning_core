## Level 0

Prompt:
A network of towns follows, with the cost of each direct stage given.
Towns: Cedarlow, Falhaven, Goldenington, Newey, Northhurst, Southey.
Falhaven <-> Goldenington costs 12.
Newey <-> Southey costs 5.
Cedarlow <-> Southey costs 7.
Falhaven <-> Northhurst costs 11.
Falhaven <-> Northhurst costs 6.
Falhaven <-> Newey costs 9.
Cedarlow <-> Falhaven costs 9.
Northhurst <-> Southey costs 5.
A shipment must go from Cedarlow to Goldenington.
Give the cheapest route as an arrow-separated list of town names that the shipment takes, for example "A -> B -> C". Among equally cheap routes the one with fewer legs wins, and if the leg count is also tied the alphabetically smaller sequence of town names wins. The answer is the route, exactly as described.

Answer:
Cedarlow -> Falhaven -> Goldenington
