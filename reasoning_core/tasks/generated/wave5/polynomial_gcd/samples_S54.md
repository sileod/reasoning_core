## Level 0

### Example

Find the monic greatest common divisor of the two polynomials below, where each coefficient is written as a reduced fraction like 3/2 or -1/4 (a whole number has no slash), and the leading term is written first with its sign. Give only the resulting polynomial as the answer.

P:
-x^2-2x

Q:
4x^2-3x

**Answer:**

x
