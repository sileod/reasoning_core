## Level 0

### Prompt

A jar starts with 8 marbles. 2 more marbles were added; then 3 more marbles were added; then 4 marbles were removed. The jar now holds 9 marbles. How many marbles were in the jar immediately after step 2? Answer with a number.


### Answer

13



### Prompt

A jar starts with 8 buttons. 4 more buttons were added; then 9 more buttons were added; then the count was cut to a third. The jar now holds 7 buttons. How many buttons were in the jar immediately after step 2? Answer with a number.


### Answer

21



## Level 2

### Prompt

A jar starts with 4 shells. The count was multiplied by 5; then the count was cut to a fifth; then the count was cut to half; then 15 more shells were added; then 14 more shells were added; then 7 more shells were added. The jar now holds 38 shells. How many shells were in the jar immediately after step 5? Answer with a number.


### Answer

31



### Prompt

The jar now holds 4 buttons. How many buttons were in the jar immediately after step 5? Answer with a number.
